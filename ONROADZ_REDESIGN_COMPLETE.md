# OnRoadz-Inspired Redesign - Complete ✅

## 🎨 Design Transformation Summary

Successfully transformed LivinLease from a dark, sophisticated theme to a clean, light OnRoadz-inspired design with subtle and simple colors.

---

## ✅ Completed Changes

### 1. Color Scheme (Subtle & Simple)
**Primary Color - Soft Orange:**
- Changed from bold `#FF6B35` to subtle `#ff7a38` (orange-600)
- More professional and less aggressive
- Used for CTAs and important actions

**Secondary Color - Calm Blue:**
- Changed from deep `#004E89` to soft `#0284c7` (blue-600)
- Trustworthy and calming
- Used for stats section and trust indicators

**Accent Color - Soft Green:**
- Changed from teal to gentle green `#22c55e`
- Used for success states and checkmarks
- Subtle and reassuring

**Background:**
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` for main background
- Light gray `#f5f5f5` for sections
- Neutral-50 `#fafafa` for subtle contrast
