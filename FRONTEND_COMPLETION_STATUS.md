- Padding: `p-4 sm:p-6 md:p-8`

### **Grid Layouts**
- Cards: `grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3 xl:grid-cols-4`
- Stats: `grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4`

### **Flexbox Patterns**
- Stack on mobile: `flex-col sm:flex-row`

- Icons: `w-4 h-4 sm:w-5 sm:h-5`
